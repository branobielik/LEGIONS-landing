The reflection strengthens when Aureole Ward forms, yet spectroscopy finds no matching emission above the water. Myzroth has ordered the pool undisturbed, treating the anomaly as a potential secondary signal rather than contamination. Vanguard command remains concerned that something beneath the cooled crust may be answering the Beacon keeper without receiving the Beacon.
