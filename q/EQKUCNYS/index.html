Industrial processing concourse · Status: Containment Pending

TOP SECRET — Field review centers on the stacked processing tower behind Kseka, where illuminated rings encircle a sealed cylindrical core. Vapor escapes at ground level, yet no corresponding pressure loss appears in the facility readout. Kseka remained within the plume while the Aegis Dome signature diverted energy toward the tower rather than away from it. The Ledger identifies the structure as recoverable infrastructure, but its accumulated charge has no assigned account. Containment is pending until engineers determine whether the barrier protected Kseka or preserved whatever remains inside the core.
