Field Log 03:07-A

Reflective Exalted Order containment chamber · Status: Containment Pending

TOP SECRET — The circular ceiling aperture above Grapi releases a fixed column of white light while the chamber's blue panels pulse independently. Grapi stood beneath the opening through several pulse sequences, and the floor reflection ceased changing for a single heartbeat each time the panels aligned. Observers classify the event as possible Epoch Cascade resonance, limited to one narrow zone and one brief interval. No mechanical shutter is visible within the aperture, and surrounding reflections continue normally. Containment remains pending because a true Still Hour cannot be distinguished from a controlled chamber test without risking interruption of the moment Grapi has sworn to hold.
