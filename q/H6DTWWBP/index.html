Field Log 03:08-A

Open colonnade within an Exalted Order enclave · Status: Unresolved

TOP SECRET — A detached circular mechanism rests against the left colonnade, its blue center lit although no cable reaches the housing. Iska positioned between the object and the open plaza, maintaining the Order's foremost duty while the mechanism emitted periodic frost-like halos across the polished floor. The halos collapsed on contact with Iska's Carbon Freeze field and returned toward their source without crossing the knight's stance. Engineers found no temperature drop on the surrounding stone. Status remains Unresolved because the device may be measuring resolve, amplifying reflected force, or waiting for a condition the Exalted Order has not authorized observers to test.
