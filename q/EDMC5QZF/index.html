Field Log 03:05-A

Mountain stream within a waterfall valley · Status: Restricted

TOP SECRET — A circular red-blue reflection persists in the shallow stream before Thryne, despite no corresponding object above the water. Thryne studies its alternating bands as if they were a record, stepping onto the central stone only after the red phase repeats. The behavior recalls Backstep Pulse discipline, with each cycle seeming to return the water to an earlier state while draining brightness from the next. Water sampled beyond the ring behaves normally, and the surrounding falls show no interruption. Access is Restricted until the Order decides whether this is a stored Still Hour, an echo, or a warning written backward.
