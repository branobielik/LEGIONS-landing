TOP SECRET — The carved balustrade beside Patro carries a repeating inscription that stops one symbol short of completing its pattern. Patro traced no mark and spoke no word, yet condensation filled the missing symbol whenever the terrace shadow touched the final panel. Observers compared the effect to Field Net behavior: an incomplete boundary that still closes around everything within it and denies recovery. The inscription's language remains unclassified, and nearby masonry carries no related text. Access is Restricted because the Order believes completion may coincide with Patro's silence ending, but no record establishes what the symbol would contain.
