Xev

Honor Warden Recruit

Archive record

Xev trains in a cavern where every wall glitters with crystal, and every shard can become a weapon. The Splinter Shot cuts the flanks with jagged fragments and pierces the enemy in the middle. Like every Honor Warden, Xev carries a bond-stone grown in that cavern, and has sworn never to let it go dark.
