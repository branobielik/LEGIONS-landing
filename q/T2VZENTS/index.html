TOP SECRET — The switchback road on the right-hand slope contains a short segment that appears twice between the same roadside posts. Order analysts recognize the logic of Haste Bloom, yet no temporal effect was recorded in the grass, pool, or distant settlement. The duplicate may be perspective, though its lane markings remain inconsistent with that explanation. The case is Under Review because Quenz described the road as early, not broken, and declined to predict which curve will become real.
