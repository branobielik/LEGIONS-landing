Autumn mountain sanctuary · Status: Under Review

TOP SECRET: The upper waterfall behind Omorivo divides around a central rock, yet the lower stream recombines without visible turbulence. Omorivo’s bond-stone shows an aged outer ring and a clear center whenever the divided water enters view. During Year Shrink resonance, fallen leaves in the stream fade to dust while leaves on the banks remain unchanged. The Warden has marked the pool for observation but refuses to remove any stone from its bed. The site is under review because the water may be measuring bonds rather than time, and Honor Warden doctrine allows no test that could break either.
